Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. Membership Cancellation

Members may cancel their membership under the following conditions:

  • Monthly Memberships: 30 days written notice required for cancellation
  • Quarterly Memberships: 30 days written notice required for cancellation
  • Annual Memberships: No refunds within first 3 months. After 3 months, pro-rated refund available with 30 days notice
  • Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via email or in person at the reception
  • Membership remains active until the notice period ends

2. Membership Refunds

Refund Eligibility

Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method. Processing fees may apply.

  • Medical Reasons: Full refund with medical documentation and doctor's recommendation
  • Relocation: Pro-rated refund with proof of relocation (more than 50km away)
  • Service Discontinuation: Full refund if we discontinue services permanently
  • Annual Memberships: Pro-rated refund after 3 months of active membership
  • Joining Fee: Non-refundable under any circumstances

3. Booking Cancellations

Cancellation policies for bookings and sessions:

  • Personal Training: 24-hour notice required for cancellation or rescheduling
  • Pool Sessions: 12-hour notice required for cancellation
  • Group Classes: 4-hour notice required for cancellation
  • No-show Policy: No refund or rescheduling for no-shows
  • Late Cancellations: May incur a 50% charge of the session fee

4. Free Trial Cancellations

Free trial bookings can be cancelled:

  • Up to 4 hours before the scheduled trial session
  • By calling our reception or sending an email
  • No penalty for cancelling free trials
  • Multiple no-shows may disqualify from future free trials

5. Membership Freeze Policy

Freeze Your Membership

Members can freeze their membership for up to 30 days per year for valid reasons.

  • Medical Freeze: Up to 60 days with medical documentation
  • Travel Freeze: Up to 30 days with travel proof
  • Personal Reasons: Up to 15 days per year
  • Freeze request must be submitted before the freeze period begins
  • Membership fees continue during freeze period at 50% rate
  • Maximum 2 freeze requests per year

6. Payment Disputes

In case of payment disputes:

  • Contact our billing department within 7 days of the transaction
  • Provide transaction details and reason for dispute
  • We will investigate and respond within 5 business days
  • Valid disputes will be resolved with full refund
  • Frivolous disputes may result in membership review

7. Refund Method

Refunds are processed as follows:

  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • Processing time: 7-10 business days
  • Bank transfers may take additional 3-5 business days
  • Credit card refunds depend on your bank's processing time
  • Refund confirmation will be sent via email

8. Non-Refundable Items

The following items are non-refundable:

  • Joining/Registration fees
  • Administrative fees
  • Locker rental fees
  • Purchased merchandise (unless defective)
  • Consumable items (supplements, drinks, etc.)
  • Special event bookings

9. Exceptional Circumstances

Velocity Elite Club reserves the right to make exceptions to this policy in exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis. Such decisions are at the sole discretion of management.

10. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or cancellation:

  • Send an email to: refunds@velocityelite.com
  • Visit our reception desk in person
  • Call our billing department: +91 98765 43210
  • Include your membership ID, contact details, and reason for request
  • Attach supporting documents (medical certificates, etc.) if applicable

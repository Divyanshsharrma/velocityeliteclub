Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between plans?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your membership plan at any time. The difference will be prorated accordingly.
Is there a joining fee?
We offer zero joining fee for all annual memberships. Monthly plans have a nominal registration fee of ₹499.
Can I freeze my membership?
Yes, you can freeze your membership for up to 30 days per year for medical reasons or travel, with proper documentation.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and EMI options through Razorpay payment gateway.
